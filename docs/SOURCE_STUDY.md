# Learn from a curriculum or textbook

Choose **Use a curriculum** or **Study a textbook** from My learning. Existing subjects provide the same workflow under Study material.

You can import an authorized PDF, Markdown or text file, paste a real outline, select an existing subject resource, or use a readable public HTTPS source. The app prepares a local outline rather than requiring a model to invent one.

Review detected chapters, modules or topics before applying them. Rename, select, reorder or add sections as needed. If automatic structure detection is unreliable, review the sections manually. Printed contents-page numbers are not assumed to be physical PDF page numbers.

Adoption creates source-linked curriculum entries without claiming understanding. Opening a section retrieves the current section and relevant source excerpts for the tutor. Different chats retain the same subject knowledge and evidence.

PDF study can open the original beside a conversation. Questions about pages or selected excerpts retain genuine source locations. Useful explanations and excerpts can be saved into notes or reviewed textbook additions.

## Practical limits

- Source-study PDFs: up to 100 MB and 2,000 pages.
- Ordinary document imports: up to 20 MB and 200 pages.
- OCR and outline detection are heuristic. Scans, complex layouts, equations and figures may require manual review.
- The app cannot teach inaccessible or unseen chapters as though it has read them. It must not invent citations.
- AI enrichment uses the selected connection and remains subject to provider availability and review.

Only import and redistribute material you have permission to use. A personal study import does not establish permission to publish the book in an exported textbook or repository.
