Electron and development tools also retain their upstream licenses. The bundled Inter font is openly licensed; its license is included in `assets/fonts/`. Proprietary system font files are not redistributed.

The project references public scientific sources such as RCSB PDB, PubChem, Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and open-access literature. Individual figures, documents and datasets retain their own licenses. Source attribution and licensing must be preserved when caching, exporting or sharing material.

Test fixtures identify public scientific provenance in `tests/fixtures/README.md`. Recorded teaching fixtures contain general educational test content, not a learner's biography or private history.

LearnHarness was inspected as an AGPL-licensed reference during early research. Its source, assets and prompts are not included in this application. The public source package excludes the cloned reference repository and reverse-engineering tool output. See [reference research](REFERENCE_RESEARCH.md).

OpenAI and ChatGPT are names of their respective owner. Learning Studio is independent and is not presented as an official OpenAI application.
